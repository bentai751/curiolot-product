CURIOLOT is a collectible-auction software platform being developed for Hong Kong by Cultivata Services Limited （培賢有限公司）.

Explore the interactive demo

What we are building

The product is designed to help collectors discover auction listings, follow bidding activity and understand transaction progress. Its categories include models, art toys, cards and other collectibles.

Listing discovery, bidding flows and bid/order tracking are development areas. The full platform is not publicly launched, and payment and transaction flows are still being validated.

What you can try here

The interactive demo lets you filter synthetic listings, select a collectible and place a simulated bid. It shows how a bidding interaction can work, without an account or payment.

All listings, prices and bid records are synthetic. This is a separate demonstration frontend, not a public deployment of the private auction application. It does not connect to live auctions, payments or customer data.
